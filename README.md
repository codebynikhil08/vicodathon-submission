# InterviewerIQ

## AI-Powered Interview Assistant

InterviewerIQ is an AI-powered interview preparation platform that helps students and job seekers practice interview questions, generate AI-powered responses, and receive feedback on their answers.

The goal of InterviewerIQ is to provide a realistic and interactive interview practice experience using AI and voice technology.

---

## Features

### AI Interview Assistant

- Ask interview-related questions.
- Get AI-generated answers using Google Gemini.
- Receive concise and interview-friendly explanations.
- Get practical examples where appropriate.

### Interview Modes

InterviewerIQ supports multiple interview types:

- Technical Interview
- HR Interview
- Behavioral Interview

The selected mode changes how the AI responds and evaluates the candidate.

### AI Answer Evaluation

Candidates can submit their answers and receive AI-powered feedback.

The evaluation includes:

- Overall Score
- Accuracy
- Clarity
- Technical Knowledge
- Strengths
- Areas for Improvement
- Feedback
- Improved Answer
